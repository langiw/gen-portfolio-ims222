About Me

Hey! I'm Jay Doe, a college student majoring in Interactive Media & Game Design. I thrive on creating fast-paced, visually punchy game mechanics and rebellious, stylized art worlds inspired by street culture, arcade classics, and modern action games.

Whether I'm scripting C# mechanics, painting key art concepts, or building intricate narrative bibles, my goal is always to deliver an unforgettable adrenaline rush to players.

Narrative Worlds

Scroll or swipe through four original universe synopses.

1. Sector 9: Splatter District

Cyberpunk / Underground

In a subterranean metropolis where color is contraband, rogue graffiti crews battle militarized megacorporations using weaponized paint-throwers to reclaim authority over the city's broadcast towers.

2. Velocity Horizon

Sci-Fi Racing / Punk

Set on an orbital ring station, outlaw speedsters pilot anti-gravity hoverbikes through crumbling solar sails, risking hyper-space collapse to win ultimate freedom for their home colony.

3. Aetherpunk Rebellion

Fantasy / Mechanical

A high-fantasy dieselpunk realm where rebellious sky-pirates harness enchanted lightning crystals to foil the tyrannical iron armada marching across floating island archipelagos.

4. Echoes of the Void

Cosmic Horror / Action

When a deep-space mining station accidentally pierces a dimension of living dark matter, a lone sound engineer must craft acoustic weapons from industrial noise gear to survive.
